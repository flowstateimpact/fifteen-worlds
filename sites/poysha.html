    The rules নিয়ম

    • Every payment rounds up to the next ten taka. An exact ten gives ten.
    • No fee to save. No fee to open the jar. An agent charges his usual rate to hand you cash, and we add nothing.
    • Your money is kept apart from ours, in a trust account at a scheduled bank.
    • The jar belongs to your number and your NID, not to the handset. Lose the phone, keep the jar.
